Project AirStorm

All projects

A weather visualization application built for Air Force Global Strike Command with LSU Shreveport and the Cyber Innovation Center. The team put Google DeepMind’s GraphCast model into service for 16-day forecasts and built a method for comparing its accuracy against the standard operational models.

I worked across the stack: the React front end, the Flask services behind it, and the Docker-based CI/CD pipeline that kept releases moving to the points of contact.
